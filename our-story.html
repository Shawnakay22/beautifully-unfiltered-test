BEHIND THE BRAND · A NOTE FROM OUR FOUNDER

A little wit.A lot of heart.

I've always believed the right words can change someone's day.

Sometimes they're heartfelt. Sometimes they're wildly inappropriate. And sometimes, when life gets complicated, they're exactly what someone needs to hear.

I've been lucky enough to have friendships filled with laughter, honest conversations, and the kind of moments that remind you how much people matter.

That's what inspired Beautifully Unfiltered.

I wanted to create something more personal than a generic greeting card. Something beautifully designed, thoughtfully written, and never afraid to have a little personality.

Because whether you're celebrating someone, making them laugh, or finding the words for a difficult moment, the sentiment should feel as special as the person receiving it.

With love and laughter,Shawna
FOR TARYN · FOREVER UNFILTERED

The Taryn Edit

My best friend Taryn was one of a kind. Bold, hilarious, wonderfully inappropriate, and completely without a filter.

She had a gift for curating the perfect greeting card. She knew how to find the one that said exactly what needed to be said—usually with a little attitude and absolutely no apologies.

It was never just about the card. It was about knowing someone well enough to find the words that would make them laugh, feel loved, or know they were understood.

That spirit helped inspire Beautifully Unfiltered, and it's the heart of The Taryn Edit—a collection created in her honor, as fearless and unforgettable as she was.

A portion of proceeds from Beautifully Unfiltered will support ovarian cancer research in Taryn's memory.
