BEHIND THE BRAND · A NOTE FROM OUR FOUNDER

A little wit.
A lot of heart.

I've always said that in my next lifetime, I want to come back as a comedian.

The irony? I'm not actually the funny one.

I'm the one surrounded by the funny ones. The girlfriends who can turn an ordinary Tuesday into a comedy show. The coworkers who make a completely ridiculous workday bearable. The friends who know exactly which wildly inappropriate meme to send when you need a laugh.

And somewhere along the way, I realized that some of the best parts of my life have been the friendships that made me laugh through all of it.

The group chats. The inside jokes. The conversations that probably shouldn't be repeated. The women who have shown up through every chapter of life, sometimes with the perfect advice and sometimes with absolutely terrible advice delivered at exactly the right moment.

Those relationships are what inspired Beautifully Unfiltered.

Because as much as I love a good meme (and believe me, I do), I started thinking about how wonderful it would be to take that connection one step further.

To send something that says, I saw this and immediately thought of you.

Something beautiful, thoughtful, a little unexpected, and just inappropriate enough to make the right person laugh out loud.

Not another generic greeting card. Something that actually sounds like us.

Of all the friendships that shaped this idea, one in particular helped me appreciate the art of finding exactly the right card.

My dear friend Taryn had a gift for it.

She loved finding that one card that made her laugh out loud, immediately think of a girlfriend, and know she absolutely had to send it. It wasn't just about finding something funny. It was about knowing someone well enough to recognize the perfect words for them.

And that, to me, is what makes a card worth sending.

Taryn also had an unforgettable laugh, a beautifully filthy mouth, and a way of turning strangers into friends. She brought so much personality into the lives of the people who knew her.

When I began imagining Beautifully Unfiltered, I kept coming back to that little ritual of hers. The joy of finding something so perfectly suited to someone you love that you simply can't leave it behind.

Her influence became part of the heart of this brand, and The Taryn Edit was created in her honor.

Beautifully Unfiltered is my way of celebrating all the women who've made life funnier, richer, and a little more interesting.

The work besties. The lifelong girlfriends. The women who've seen us at our best, our worst, and occasionally our most unhinged.

Because sometimes tagging someone in a meme just isn't enough.

Sometimes you want to remind them that they're worth a little extra thought.

And if that reminder makes them laugh so hard they nearly spit out their coffee?

Even better.

With love, laughter, and a little inappropriate humor,
Shawna
Founder, Beautifully Unfiltered
