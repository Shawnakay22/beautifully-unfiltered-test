The Taryn Edit

For Taryn.

She turned strangers into friends, never turned down the volume on her laugh, and faced life's hardest battles with a heart full of love and a beautifully filthy mouth.

Some people leave memories. She left stories worth telling.

Taryn believed there was an art to finding the perfect card for someone. Not just any card. The one that made you laugh out loud, immediately think of a girlfriend, and know you had to send it.

It was never just about the card. It was about knowing someone well enough to find the words that were perfectly, intentionally theirs.

The Taryn Edit was created in her honor. A collection as funny, fearless, and beautifully inappropriate as the woman who inspired it.

In honor of Taryn, 10% of profits from The Taryn Edit collection will be donated to ovarian cancer research.
